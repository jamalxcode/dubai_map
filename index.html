    How to read the map

    Each label shows a community's median annual rent (or the measure you pick) at an approximate community point, not a building. Where communities are too close to read apart, they merge into one dark label with the average, weighted by number of contracts; click it to zoom in. Click a white label for the unit-by-unit breakdown.
