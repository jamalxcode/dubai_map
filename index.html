Reading the map

Each label is an area's median yearly rent for the property type you picked. Where areas sit too close to read, they merge into one blue label with the average, weighted by the number of contracts. Tap it to zoom in; tap a white label for the breakdown by unit.

Labels sit at an approximate reference point for each area, not on a building.

    What this is not

    Not asking prices from listing sites, and not the RERA rental index used for rent increases. An independent tool, not an official DLD or RERA service.
