Reading the map

Each label shows an area's typical yearly rent (its median) for the home you picked, on a badge in its price colour. Where areas sit too close to read, they merge into one label named after the busiest area with how many more it covers ("JLT + 12 areas"), showing their contract-weighted average. Tap it to zoom in; tap a single area's label for the breakdown by unit.

Labels sit at an approximate reference point for each area, not on a building.

In Hotels and Holiday homes each label is an area's typical price a night (the median of its places with a price); dashed labels rest on fewer than 3 places. Solid dots have a price; hollow rings (from street level) are places with none on booking sites.

    What this is not

    Not asking prices from listing sites, and not the RERA rental index used for rent increases. An independent tool, not an official DLD or RERA service.

    Hotel and holiday home prices are samples (the lowest offer for two nights two to three weeks ahead), not live booking prices for your dates: the booking site sets the price.
